# Retracted Paper Analysis

## 1. Paper metadata

**Original paper:** Guo, X., Dong, L., and Hao, D. *Cellular functions of spermatogonial stem cells in relation to JAK/STAT signaling pathway.*  
**Venue:** Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology  
**Original DOI:** `10.3389/fcell.2023.1339390`  
**Publication date:** 13 February 2024  
**Retraction date:** 16 February 2024  
**Retraction DOI:** `10.3389/fcell.2024.1386861`  
**Retraction status verified:** 2 August 2026 against the publisher record.

## 2. The violation

The appropriate classification is **fabrication or scientifically invalid AI-generated figures**, combined with inadequate validation of AI-assisted content. The publisher did not state that the authors fabricated experimental data, so the classification should not be broadened beyond the documented evidence.

The official notice states that concerns were raised about the AI-generated figures and concludes that the article failed the journal's standards of scientific rigor. The decisive short passage is: **“concerns were raised regarding the nature of its AI-generated figures.”**

The case demonstrates that disclosure alone is not a sufficient defense. A disclosed tool can still produce false, anatomically impossible, or meaningless material. Scientific responsibility remains with the authors. In a review article, figures still make knowledge claims: they represent biological structures and mechanisms and therefore require the same verification as prose, tables, and quantitative results.

## 3. Detection

The publisher credits concerned readers who contacted the journal after publication. The timeline is unusually short: the article was retracted three days after publication. The failure could have been detected at several earlier points.

The authors should have checked every label, anatomical structure, and biological relationship before submission. Co-authors should have inspected the final figures during approval. A subject-matter reviewer could have compared the illustrations against established anatomy and the cited literature. The handling editor and production process also had opportunities to question visibly incoherent labels or structures.

The detection pathway matters because it shows that ordinary visual scrutiny, not advanced forensic software, was sufficient. The problem passed through author review, editorial handling, and peer review, but was identified by post-publication readers.

## 4. Systemic analysis

The retraction notice documents the immediate problem but does not explain why the safeguards failed. The following interpretation is therefore an inference rather than a publisher finding.

One enabling factor may be the treatment of figures in review articles as decorative summaries instead of evidence-bearing components. Another is reviewer and editor workload, which can encourage attention to narrative flow while visual claims receive less scrutiny. Generative tools also create a misleading appearance of professionalism: high-resolution graphics can look credible even when their content is nonsensical.

A deeper structural issue is that disclosure policies often focus on whether AI was used rather than whether the output was independently validated. A checkbox saying that an image tool was used does not establish correctness, provenance, licensing, or reproducibility. The governance question should therefore be: who verified each scientific claim, using what evidence, and where is that verification recorded?

## 5. Forensic check

A numerical forensic test such as Benford's law or GRIM is not suitable for the central issue because this case concerns generated figures rather than a reported numerical dataset. The correct forensic control is a provenance and content-validation audit.

For every figure, the audit should record the source of each element, the tool and version used, whether the image is illustrative or evidentiary, the person who verified it, and the literature or raw evidence used for validation. A model-generated anatomical figure should be rejected unless a qualified expert confirms every labelled structure and relationship.
